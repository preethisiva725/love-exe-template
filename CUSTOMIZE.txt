========================================================
              💕 LOVE.EXE TEMPLATE 💕
          A Little Something For Your Person
========================================================


WELCOME! 🥹❤️

You downloaded the LOVE.EXE romantic website template!

You DON'T need to understand HTML, CSS, or JavaScript.

Just follow the steps below.


========================================================
                    📁 YOUR FILES
========================================================

Your folder should contain:

index.html
style.css
script.js
music.mp3

That's it!

You can add your own photos later.


========================================================
              ✨ STEP 1 — CHANGE THE NAME
========================================================

Open:

index.html

Press:

Ctrl + F

Search for:

YOUR PERSON

You will find:

YOUR PERSON
DETECTED

Replace YOUR PERSON with the name of your person.

Example:

ARJUN
DETECTED

or:

MY LOVE
DETECTED


========================================================
             💌 STEP 2 — CHANGE THE MESSAGES
========================================================

Open:

index.html

You can change the romantic message on the HEART screen.

Look for:

This is the part of me
that somehow belongs to you.

and change it to whatever you want.


You can also change:

My heart's been a little heavy today,
thinking of you 🥺


Make the message completely yours!


========================================================
             💕 STEP 3 — CHANGE SECRET MESSAGES
========================================================

Open:

script.js

Search for:

const secretMessages

You will find several messages.

Example:

"You make me smile without even trying. ❤️"

Change them to your own messages.

You can write anything:

"You're my favorite notification. 💕"

"I still smile when I see your name."

"You make everything better."


========================================================
             📸 STEP 4 — ADD YOUR PHOTOS
========================================================

Add 8 photos to this folder.

Rename them exactly:

photo1.jpg
photo2.jpg
photo3.jpg
photo4.jpg
photo5.jpg
photo6.jpg
photo7.jpg
photo8.jpg

IMPORTANT:

The photo names must match EXACTLY.

Put the photos in the SAME folder as:

index.html


========================================================
              🎵 STEP 5 — CHANGE THE MUSIC
========================================================

The template already includes:

music.mp3

If you want your own song:

1. Choose your audio file.
2. Rename it:

music.mp3

3. Replace the existing music.mp3.

IMPORTANT:

Keep the filename:

music.mp3


========================================================
             📝 STEP 6 — CHANGE PHOTO CAPTIONS
========================================================

Open:

index.html

Search for:

one of my favorite little moments ♡

and change the captions underneath each photo.

Make them personal!


========================================================
             💖 STEP 7 — CHANGE FINAL MESSAGE
========================================================

Open:

index.html

Find:

If I could choose again...

I'd still choose you.

Change this into your own final message.

For example:

"If I had a thousand lives,
I'd find you in every one."


========================================================
              ▶️ STEP 8 — RUN THE WEBSITE
========================================================

If you use VS Code:

1. Open the LOVE-EXE-TEMPLATE folder.
2. Open index.html.
3. Right-click index.html.
4. Select:

Open with Live Server

Your website will open in the browser.


========================================================

After adding your photos:

Test it on your phone before sharing it.
